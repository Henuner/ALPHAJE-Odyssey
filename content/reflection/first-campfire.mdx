## A beginning that stays open

Some websites try to look complete from the first day. This one should feel different.

It should be allowed to have unfinished corners, quiet notes, and visible traces of learning.
The important thing is not perfection; it is continuity.

## What I want to remember

The site is a promise to keep observing:

- What I learn
- What I make
- What surprises me
- What changes my mind

## A small rule

When something feels difficult, leave a record. Future ALPHAJE may need that map.
